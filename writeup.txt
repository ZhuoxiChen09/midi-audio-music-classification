Task 1:
Soft-vote ensemble of (a) a balanced HistGradientBoostingClassifier on a 280-dim handcrafted MIDI feature vector (chroma, duration- and velocity-weighted chroma, melodic interval and IOI histograms, velocity bins, log-duration bins, pitch-class transition matrix, Krumhansl–Kessler key fits, polyphony and tempo/time-signature scalars) and (b) a 5-layer Transformer encoder (d_model=192, 6 heads, GELU, pre-norm) over per-note token embeddings — pitch + log-binned duration + log-binned inter-onset interval + velocity bin + program family — with a [CLS] token, masked-mean + CLS pooling, class-balanced label-smoothed cross-entropy, and a chronological tail holdout for model selection.

Task 2:
Logit-averaged ensemble of (a) an XGB + HistGradientBoosting + RandomForest stack on pair features (per-segment head/tail/half-split summaries, tail-to-head and reverse "bridge" features, full-segment pitch-class histograms with abs-difference and product, and 4× metadata blocks including tempo, time signature, ticks-per-beat, program, and span) and (b) a 4-layer Transformer (d_model=192, 6 heads) over the concatenated sequence [CLS][SEPa] segA_notes [SEPb] segB_notes with per-token segment embeddings, trained with synthetic swap augmentation and predicted with forward + reverse logit averaging.

Task 3:
Probability-averaged ensemble of (a) a 3-seed ResNet34 (ImageNet-pretrained) tagger on log-mel spectrograms with 1st/2nd-order deltas (128 mels, hop 512, 10 s clips), trained with asymmetric loss + pos-weighted BCE, mixup + cutmix, SpecAugment, AdamW + cosine schedule with frozen-backbone warmup, EMA model averaging, and time-shift test-time augmentation, and (b) the AudioSet-pretrained Audio Spectrogram Transformer (MIT/ast-finetuned-audioset-10-10-0.4593) fine-tuned with a fresh 10-way multilabel head, BCE-with-logits loss, SpecAugment-style time/frequency masking, frozen-backbone warmup, and AdamW + cosine schedule with separate head and backbone learning rates.
